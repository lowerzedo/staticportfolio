# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and engineering leaders assessing Nazar Hasanov for cloud engineering, backend engineering, DevOps, SRE, solutions architecture, and adjacent software roles. They are usually scanning quickly, often during shortlisting, and need an immediate sense of technical range followed by credible evidence.

## Product Purpose

Present Nazar as a technically authoritative and versatile software engineer, make his current role and experience easy to understand, and turn interest into a LinkedIn visit, GitHub visit, or direct conversation. Success means a visitor understands his value within the first screen and can verify his experience, expertise, and credentials without friction.

Selected projects follow Credentials and Experience: CAI Verify, SyncFlo (the native Swift iOS app), MedBot, Kaizen Flow, Wilsons Creek Insurance (Insurance Management), and Sterling Ledger Capital (Loan Management). Visitors can view product screenshots, scan the purpose and stack, then expand technical details. Copy must reflect implemented capabilities and distinguish engineering alphas and reference implementations from production applications. Source and asset provenance is recorded in PROJECTS.md.

## Brand Personality

Technically authoritative, ambitious, and personable. The experience should feel engineered, kinetic, and assured: creative enough to be memorable, disciplined enough to build trust.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer portfolio templates, decorative glassmorphism, terminal-themed dark interfaces, repetitive card grids, skill progress bars, stock code imagery, and motion that delays access to content. Do not copy Nothin', PX Push, or Awwwards portfolio conventions literally; use their confidence, scale, and spatial choreography as inspiration only.

## Design Principles

1. Make the value clear before asking the visitor to explore.
2. Treat motion as spatial explanation, not decoration.
3. Put verifiable evidence ahead of broad self-description.
4. Balance creative confidence with fast recruiter scanning.
5. Keep every interaction usable without motion, pointer precision, or color alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, landmarks, focus visibility, and form behavior. Respect reduced-motion preferences with an equivalent static composition, maintain useful content without JavaScript, and avoid interactions that depend solely on hover or color.
